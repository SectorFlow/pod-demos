How a SectorFlow pod works

A pod is a small set of AI agents built for one job in your business, running in a private space of its own. Your team stays in charge of every decision that matters. This page applies to every pod we build.

What stays yours

Your systems and your dataThe pod reads from them and writes back only where you have approved. Nothing is copied into a product you do not control.
Every decisionApprovals, edits and rejections are made by your team, in your portal or in the channel you already use.
Your rulesPolicies, limits, terms and calendars change only when you change them. No agent can edit a rule.
On exitYour records, the audit log, the rules and the documentation come with you, with a transition period to hand over.

Where it runs, and how your data is kept apart

LayerWhat it isHow you are separated
Your podYour agents, steps, messages, tools, test cases and metrics, in a repository and container that exist only for you.Nothing in it is shared. Another client's pod cannot see it or call it.
Shared platformThe services under every pod: model access, knowledge, connections, quality gate, tracing, sign-in, versions. One platform, run and patched by SectorFlow.Every record carries your tenant id and the database enforces it on every query (row-level security). Your users see only your tenant; so do your agents.
Your systemsERP, CRM, WMS, phone, email, ticketing. Unchanged.Reached only through Connections, with keys held in the platform, read by default and written only where you have approved.
Data regionWhere records, snapshots and backups live.Chosen per client (Canada, US or EU) and confirmed in writing before go-live.

How changes reach you

ChangeHow it goes live
Content you ownRules, terms, calendars, messages and knowledge are saved in the portal and apply on the next run. Every change is versioned and in the audit log.
How an agent behavesA new version is built, then replayed against real cases from your own history. It only goes live after you have seen the result, and the previous version stays one step away.
The AI model itselfModels are swapped behind the pod without a rebuild, and go through the same gate as any other change.

What we never do

Train on your dataThe model provider is under contract with no training and no retention. Your data is not used to improve anything for anyone else.
Share or sell itNo advertisers, data brokers, analytics vendors or other clients, in any form.
Let an agent act alone where it mattersMoney, customers and records in your systems wait for a person. Where you choose to automate, the limits are yours and logged.
Ship an untested changeEvery version passes your own cases first. A worse version is blocked, and no one can override the gate.

Hosting region, retention periods and the list of processors are confirmed in writing for each client before go-live.
